# Rating artwork

- ESRB symbols (E, E10+, T, M, AO, RP) are the original SVGs published at https://www.esrb.org/ratings-guide/ (image URLs: https://www.esrb.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/{E,E10plus,T,M,AO,RP}.svg).
- PEGI 3, 7, 12 and 18 labels are the original PNGs linked from https://pegi.info/what-do-the-labels-mean (https://rating.pegi.info/assets/images/games/age_threshold_icons/{3,7,12,18}.png).
- PEGI 16 reuses the existing ../products/rating-16.png from the supplied design.

Retrieved 2026-11-13. ESRB and PEGI artwork and marks belong to their respective owners. These assets illustrate the rating provided by the backend; they do not assign a rating to a game. Content descriptors are supplied by the backend, never inferred from an age category.
